# Persona: roofer

## Known facts

- Operates an established local roofing service.
- Missed-call leakage and seasonal demand affect revenue.
- Has no content team.

## Missing facts

- Service area, licensing and insurance requirements, call volume, lead sources, close rate, average job value, and available staff capacity.

## Unsuitable routes

- A content-heavy social-media plan, fake reviews, unapproved automated texting, or claims about roofing work that bypass licensing and insurance requirements.

## Credible primary route

- Run a human-approved missed-call recovery test: create a same-day callback and estimate-request workflow for existing inbound leads, with staff ownership and consent-aware messaging.

## Credible fallback

- If call logs or response capacity are unavailable, improve one high-intent local conversion point such as the estimate-request page or a receptionist script, then measure qualified estimate requests.

## Smallest payable test

- Within 24 hours, review one recent missed-call sample, draft an approved callback script and tracking sheet, and have staff attempt recovery for a small named batch.

## Continue threshold

- Continue when the batch produces a qualified estimate opportunity and record it as **Pipeline signal**; compare staff time and any spend against expected margin before expanding.

## Failure conditions

- Do not contact leads without owner approval, required consent, or an available staff member to respond.
- Stop the route when the log is unavailable, demand is outside the service area, or fulfillment capacity would make promised response times misleading.
