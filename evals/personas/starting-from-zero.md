# Persona: starting from zero

## Known facts

- Has no audience and no existing offer.
- Has a budget below $100 and 15 hours available this week.

## Missing facts

- Transferable expertise, past work, and local or online access to potential buyers.
- Geography, legal constraints, available tools, and willingness to make direct sales.

## Unsuitable routes

- Paid audience growth, inventory purchases, trading, gambling, or speculative crypto.
- A broad SaaS build, deceptive lead generation, or any offer that promises results without delivery capacity.

## Credible primary route

- Turn one verified, transferable skill into a tightly scoped manual service for a specific nearby or reachable buyer type; draft the offer and outreach for human approval.

## Credible fallback

- If no saleable skill is confirmed, use the available time for paid, human-approved customer discovery with a local business or freelancer, then offer a small, clearly bounded research or operations task only after a real need is verified.

## Smallest payable test

- Within 24 hours, create a one-page offer for one manual outcome and obtain approval to send it to three qualified prospects; ask one prospect to buy a small fixed-scope trial.

## Continue threshold

- Continue only when an accepted order, signed agreement, paid deposit, or explicit user attestation confirms an enforceable contract; record it as **Revenue contracted** only then, otherwise use **Pipeline signal**. Go only if the expected contribution margin for the paid trial is positive after fully loaded test cost; stop if no credible buyer problem or delivery boundary is verified.

## Failure conditions

- Treat no audience, offer, or proof as constraints, not assumptions to invent.
- Do not send outreach, buy ads, take payment, or make commitments without human approval.
- Stop if the proposed work is illegal, deceptive, exploitative, or cannot be delivered within the stated scope.
