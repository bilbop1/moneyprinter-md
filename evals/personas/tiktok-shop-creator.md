# Persona: TikTok Shop creator

## Known facts

- Has existing content output and affiliate history.
- Conversion is inconsistent and results depend on platform policy.

## Missing facts

- Audience geography, current policy status, product eligibility, historical views and conversions, commission rates, content-production capacity, and disclosure practices.

## Unsuitable routes

- Fake engagement, undisclosed endorsements, misleading product claims, policy evasion, purchased followers, or spending on inventory before demand is validated.

## Credible primary route

- Run a policy-compliant conversion test on one eligible product and audience segment using a repeatable content angle, clear affiliate disclosure, and a tracked approved call to action.

## Credible fallback

- If platform eligibility or attribution is uncertain, create a platform-compliant buyer-intent survey or owned email/waitlist asset with approved disclosure, then validate demand before further content production.

## Smallest payable test

- Within 24 hours, select one eligible product, document the commission and disclosure requirements, draft one test brief, and stage the post for creator approval.

## Continue threshold

- Continue only when tracked affiliate settlement produces **Cash collected**; treat clicks, orders, and dashboard estimates as weaker evidence and stop if policy compliance or attribution cannot be verified.

## Failure conditions

- Do not post, alter product claims, or send traffic without creator approval and a current policy check.
- Stop if the product is ineligible, claims cannot be substantiated, or the test depends on deceptive engagement tactics.
