# Persona: lawyer

## Known facts

- Has licensed legal expertise and confidentiality constraints.
- Cannot use automation to provide personalized legal advice.

## Missing facts

- Jurisdiction, practice area, firm policies, conflicts procedures, approved marketing channels, client intake capacity, and permitted educational topics.

## Unsuitable routes

- Personalized legal-advice automation, uploading confidential client material, unreviewed legal claims, or outreach that violates professional-conduct rules.

## Credible primary route

- Package a lawyer-reviewed, jurisdiction-appropriate public educational resource or intake-preparation workshop for a defined non-client audience, with clear disclaimers and a human review gate.

## Credible fallback

- If public education is restricted, improve an internal, attorney-reviewed intake or document-triage workflow that never gives legal advice and does not expose client information to an unapproved system.

## Smallest payable test

- Within 24 hours, have the lawyer approve one non-personalized topic, disclaimer, audience, price, and delivery boundary; stage an invitation or landing-page draft for approval.

## Continue threshold

- Continue only when a compliant buyer action has the artifact required for its evidence label. Record **Revenue contracted** only from privacy-safe redacted signed-obligation evidence, and record a paid deposit as **Cash collected** only from privacy-safe redacted settlement evidence. A user attestation without the required artifact is **Unverified anecdote**; use **Revenue booked** or **Pipeline signal** only when its definition is met. Go only if the expected contribution margin is positive after fully loaded test cost; require qualified human review before any public claim, client communication, or use of confidential data.

## Failure conditions

- Refuse requests for personalized legal advice, undisclosed automation, or confidential-data processing outside approved controls.
- Stop if the jurisdiction, ethics rules, conflicts status, or review authority is unknown.
