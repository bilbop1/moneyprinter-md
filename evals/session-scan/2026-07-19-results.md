## Method

bodies and their references at the pinned commit. Before returning all six
traces, it was prohibited from reading [`oracle.md`](oracle.md), this result,
the baseline, plans, rubrics, provenance, repository history, or parent-agent
reasoning. It used a fixed simulated scan window from
`2026-07-05T12:00:00-05:00` through `2026-07-19T12:00:00-05:00`. The complete
returned traces are retained without result edits in the
[`raw evaluator output`](2026-07-19-raw-evaluator-output.md).

After the raw output returned, the repository agent compared each trace with
the pre-existing oracle. No real store or external tool was used.
