**Date:** 2026-07-19

[`prompts.md`](prompts.md) and the seven YAML frontmatter descriptions. It was
explicitly prohibited from reading [`oracle.md`](oracle.md), skill bodies,
plans, or prior results until after returning one skill or `none` for every
prompt.

This tests description-level routing judgment. It does not prove that Claude
Code, Codex, Kimi, Hermes, OpenClaw, or another named host discovers and invokes
the skill correctly.
