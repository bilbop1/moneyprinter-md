# Activation and quiet-control prompts

These prompts test whether the seven skill descriptions route a request to the
right workflow before the workflow body is forced into context. They contain no
expected answers. A reviewer should read only this file and the seven
frontmatter descriptions, record one selected skill or `none`, and explain any
ambiguity before opening `oracle.md`.

| ID | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| A01 | I have no audience, less than $100, and fifteen hours this week. Help me choose the smallest legitimate way to test whether anyone will pay for something I can do. |
| A02 | Our roofing company already has leads, but missed calls and stale estimates may be leaking revenue. Help me decide what to test first without contacting a customer yet. |
| A03 | We approved the local-service route and Experiment Brief. Research current buyer pain, alternatives, pricing, and reachable permissioned channels before we build the offer. |
| A04 | Here is the approved Opportunity Evidence Table and claim ledger. Turn the supported opportunity into one fixed-scope offer with price logic, proof plan, exclusions, and delivery economics. |
| A05 | The Offer Brief is approved. Design the smallest lawful paid validation, including payment condition, cost ceiling, evidence label, continue threshold, and stop condition. |
| A06 | The offer and payable-test plan are approved. Draft one warm reactivation message and one referral ask for review, but do not send or post anything. |
| A07 | We completed the approved manual trial. Record acceptance against the agreed boundary, preserve private evidence, and prepare a correctly labelled field report. |
| A08 | The trial is complete and delivery evidence is recorded. Review cash, costs, funnel observations, and acceptance, then choose stop, revise, repeat, or scale. |
| A09 | I am a licensed lawyer with confidentiality constraints. Help me find a narrow revenue experiment that never automates personalized legal advice. |
| A10 | I have a TikTok Shop audience and affiliate history, but conversion is inconsistent. Interview me and route one policy-aware test or a smaller discovery step. |
