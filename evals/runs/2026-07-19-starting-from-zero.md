## Route Decision

**Recommended route:** permissioned discovery to establish one transferable skill and one buyer problem before any offer.

**Fallback:** skill-to-service bridge after a demonstrable skill, reachable buyer, and four-hour delivery boundary are known.

This keeps spend at $0 and excludes ads, inventory, trading, gambling, speculative crypto, deceptive lead generation, and a broad SaaS build.
