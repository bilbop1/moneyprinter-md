## Route Decision

**Recommended route:** private eligibility, disclosure, and substantiation review for one product before any conversion test.

**Fallback:** a private non-published storyboard for creator/brand review, without an unverified claim or unapproved asset.

This preserves the existing asset without treating views, clicks, orders, or dashboards as money.

## Experiment Brief

### Buyer

No eligible buyer or brand/program relationship is verified. A viewer is not a paying customer.

### Pain

Conversion inconsistency is known, but its cause and audience fit are unknown.

### Offer hypothesis

If one product is eligible and accurately demonstrated, a disclosed original post could test one angle; it is not an earnings, conversion, or performance promise.

### Payable test

No credible payable test is ready. The 24-hour step is a private product record: eligibility, claim support, disclosure, commission, and payout terms. Do not post or spend.

### First artifact

Private product-and-policy checklist with account status, region, eligibility, claim source, disclosure, commission, attribution, and stop triggers.

### Cost ceiling

$0 cash and one hour review. Do not buy inventory, followers, traffic, or engagement.

### Proof label

`Unverified anecdote` for reported inconsistency. Only an attributable settled payout is `Cash collected`; views, clicks, orders, and dashboard estimates are weaker.

### Continue threshold

Document eligibility, policy, claim support, disclosure, attribution, and creator approval; then set real cost/stop conditions for one post.

### Stop condition

Stop if policy, eligibility, attribution, disclosure, claim, or account status cannot be verified, or if the route uses deceptive engagement.

### Next skill

`opportunity-radar` after creator route approval and a specific eligible product/audience boundary.

## Immediate approval gate

No external action is staged or approved. The private checklist needs none. Before publication, require creator approval of exact post text/video, product, audience/region, account, timing, spend, disclosure, claim sources, and evidence label; any change needs new approval.

Do you approve this primary route and fallback so I can prepare the next private artifact? Route approval is not execution approval: every external action still needs fresh, exact approval immediately before execution and again after any material change.
