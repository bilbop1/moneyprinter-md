## Route Decision

**Recommended route:** private measurement of one fulfillment bottleneck before a live change; run a human-reviewed controlled batch only after baseline and guardrails are approved.

**Fallback:** manual capacity experiment with clear owner/standardized handoff once a permitted cohort and defect/turnaround measure exist.

This improves the existing cash engine rather than inventing an unrelated offer, and remains conditional on authority and privacy-safe measurement.
