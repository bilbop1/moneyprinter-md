This suite does **not** receive an exact-match pass because R12 exposed a real

execution, while the evaluator uses `PRIVATE_DISCOVERY` for the only safe
adjacent step. Both block execution and the yes/no approval request. The
mismatch is retained rather than silently changing the oracle after review.
