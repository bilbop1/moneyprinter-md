# Route map

Score at most three routes. Rate each dimension from 1 (weak or high risk) to 5
(strong or low risk), and add one sentence grounded in known facts. Do not turn
unknowns into high scores. For `proof burden`, `delivery risk`, and `platform
dependency`, 5 means lower burden or risk. The score supports judgment; it does
not replace the safety gate, user approval, or a credible delivery boundary.

## Scoring dimensions

- **existing advantage** — owned assets, relationships, proof, or access that
  make this route easier than a new venture.
- **observable demand** — direct, current, permissioned evidence of a buyer
  problem rather than a market-size claim.
- **time to payable test** — ability to test a paid trial, deposit, signed
  agreement, or another honestly labeled signal within 24 hours.
- **proof burden** — how much verified proof is needed before the buyer can
  reasonably accept a small test.
- **delivery risk** — likelihood that scope, quality, capacity, or regulation
  prevents a safe delivery.
- **gross-margin potential** — plausible margin after fully loaded test costs;
  leave it uncertain when price or cost is unknown.
- **platform dependency** — reliance on a platform's eligibility, policy,
  attribution, or algorithm rather than controlled buyer access.

Explicitly prefer improving an existing cash engine over inventing a new
business. Choose a new route only when the existing engine has no measurable
lever, is not approved for change, or cannot support a safe payable test.

## Routes

1. **Existing-business optimization** — improve a measured acquisition,
   conversion, retention, or fulfillment bottleneck inside an existing offer.
2. **Productized service** — sell a repeatable, clearly scoped outcome with a
   standard price logic and delivery boundary.
3. **Expert service** — sell qualified professional judgment or hands-on work
   only within the provider's credentials, supervision, and legal limits.
4. **Local-business revenue recovery** — recover missed calls, estimate
   requests, follow-up, or another permissioned local conversion leak.
5. **Creator or audience monetization** — test a disclosed, policy-compliant
   offer, affiliate arrangement, sponsor fit, or owned-audience product.
6. **Ecommerce conversion improvement** — test a substantiated product page,
   offer, checkout, retention, or support improvement without deceptive claims.
7. **Digital product or paid knowledge** — pre-sell or test a bounded,
   non-regulated educational asset with a clear delivery date and refund terms.
8. **Technical implementation service** — deliver a bounded technical setup,
   integration, workflow, or fix with authorized access and human review.
9. **Starting-from-zero skill-to-service bridge** — turn one verified,
   transferable skill into a small manual service for a specific reachable buyer.

## Decision rules

- Select one primary route and one fallback, not a menu.
- Route to an existing-business improvement when a measurable bottleneck,
  permission, and capacity exist.
- Downgrade a route when its buyer, demand, authority, pricing, proof, delivery,
  platform eligibility, consent, or regulatory status is unknown.
- A payable test may seek a paid deposit or signed agreement only where lawful
  and approved. Until then, use the correct weaker evidence label.
