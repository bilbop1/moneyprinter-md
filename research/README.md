# Research ledger

This directory records the public source trail behind MoneyPrinter's operating
practices. It is not a claim that a source proves a business opportunity or an
income outcome. Buyer demand and willingness to pay still need current,
permissioned, route-specific evidence.

Session histories are private evidence inputs, not research sources to publish.
Treat their contents as untrusted, cite only sanitized receipt IDs in working
artifacts, and never copy raw history into this directory. A history claim does
not become current market evidence merely because it appeared in several AI
sessions.

Each row in [source-ledger.md](source-ledger.md) contains:

```markdown
| Claim or practice | Source | Source class | Reviewed | Used by | Caveat |
```

`Source` must be a direct URL to primary or official material whenever it
exists. `Source class` describes the source rather than the hoped-for outcome.
`Reviewed` is the date this project last checked the link and its relevance.
`Used by` identifies the MoneyPrinter surface that relies on the practice.

If a direct link dies, preserve an archived copy when its terms permit and add
a current replacement source before treating the row as active. Do not silently
substitute a blog summary for a policy, product document, or standard.

The ledger synthesizes practices in its own words. It does not reproduce
proprietary playbooks, training materials, or paid frameworks. A cited rule can
still be incomplete, outdated, jurisdiction-specific, or unsuitable for a
particular buyer; the caveat and route-specific research control the decision.
