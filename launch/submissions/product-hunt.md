# Product Hunt

## Verdict

**P3 — weak fit for this release. Do not prioritize.** Product Hunt's current
featuring guidelines explicitly list templates among products it does not
feature. MoneyPrinter.md is intentionally a text-only skill pack, so an
otherwise polished submission is unlikely to receive the organic homepage
visibility the user wants.

**Status:** intentionally blocked. No draft or launch has been created.

## Current official submission path

A personal account can use Product Hunt's `Post` flow, add the direct product
URL, title, tagline, topics, pricing, thumbnail, gallery, description, makers,
and first comment.

Sources: [official featuring guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines)
and [official posting guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product).

## Prerequisites

For reconsideration, MoneyPrinter should become a genuinely interactive digital
product rather than a landing page around downloadable text:

- Live product people can use immediately.
- Clear functionality beyond static templates or guides.
- Personal Product Hunt account.
- Square thumbnail and at least two gallery images.
- Direct product URL and maker profile.
- Owner available for launch-day discussion.

## Listing title

> MoneyPrinter.md

## Tagline

> The receipts-first revenue operating system for AI agents

## Short description

> Interview your real starting point, choose one bounded revenue experiment,
> and keep cash separate from pipeline, estimates, and simulations.

## Long description

> MoneyPrinter.md is an open-source pack of seven Agent Skills for legitimate
> revenue experiments. A short interview routes the user to one bounded payable
> test and one fallback; focused workflows then guide research, offer design,
> permissioned acquisition, delivery proof, and cashflow review. Results stay
> labelled as cash, contracted, booked, pipeline, estimated, simulated, or
> anecdotal, and external actions always require human approval.

## Maker comment outline

If the product later becomes eligible, the owner should explain:

- why generic AI money prompts fail different users;
- how the seven evidence labels work;
- what the interactive product lets a user do immediately;
- what remains unproven;
- why external actions require approval.

The voluntary 1% pledge belongs at the end, not in the opening.

## Tags / topics

Use only the closest current topics, likely `Artificial Intelligence`,
`Productivity`, and `Open Source`.

## Links required

- Interactive product URL as primary.
- GitHub as a secondary link.

## Assets required

- Square 240×240 thumbnail.
- Two or more 1270×760 gallery images.
- Optional walkthrough video or interactive demo.

## Expected discovery mechanism

All-feed listing and, only if Product Hunt selects it, homepage featuring. A
submission can remain visible without being featured, but that is not the
desired high-impact discovery path.

## Review risks

- Templates are explicitly excluded from homepage featuring.
- A landing page plus files may be treated as a guide, template, or incomplete
  product.
- Coordinated or incentivized upvotes violate Product Hunt's rules.
- Building an unnecessary UI just for directory eligibility can weaken the
  product.

## Final human action

Skip Product Hunt for the text-only release. Revisit only after the project has
an interactive, immediately usable product surface that stands on its own.
