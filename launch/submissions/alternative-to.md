# AlternativeTo

## Verdict

**P3 — weak and likely ineligible in the current form.** AlternativeTo lists
applications and says it generally does not approve guides/tutorials, basic AI
tools, AI wrappers, or several other low-distinction categories. A text-first
skill pack may not qualify as an application.

**Status:** intentionally blocked. No application has been suggested.

## Current official submission path

After an account is at least one week old, use the user menu's **Suggest new
application** action, complete platforms, license, descriptions, and tags, then
submit for admin review.

Source: [AlternativeTo official FAQ](https://alternativeto.net/faq/).

## Prerequisites

- A standalone, released application available in English.
- Official website and source/download route.
- Account at least one week old.
- Clear comparison category and real alternatives.
- Evidence that the product is not merely a guide, template, or thin AI wrapper.

## Platforms

Do not claim operating-system or host platforms until AlternativeTo's form and
the repository's host tests support them. “Web” is not accurate for the
text-only repository merely because it has a landing page.

## Review risks

- The current package may be rejected as a guide/template rather than software.
- New accounts cannot submit for one week.
- Descriptions cannot contain promotional links.
- Inventing comparison products would undermine the listing.

## Final human action

Skip for this release. Revisit after an interactive application exists and only
if it has honest, user-meaningful alternatives.
